# FIN-2026-051: wrong tax on Orchard Lane

From: M. Iyer, Tax

Orchard Lane's invoices (KC-2026-02-C14 and earlier) carry UK VAT. They're invoiced to a London
office but everything they use runs in Texas, and that's what our 2025 tax policy keys on. The
tax line needs to be Texas sales tax. Please also make sure the restatement fixes January.
